# v18.5.1 Environmental Co-Evolution (Experimental Recovery Attempt)

## Purpose

This experiment attempted to recover from the excessive inheritance collapse observed in the initial v18.5.0 attempt.

The previous version weakened inheritance reuse too aggressively, causing adaptive novelty to dominate the system and destabilize co-evolution balance.

v18.5.1 introduced:

- partial inheritance recovery
- adaptive novelty moderation
- coevolution balancing
- continuity-safe exploration
- bounded lineage decay

while preserving:

- lightweight execution
- smartphone-compatible architecture
- bounded memory
- local-only adaptation
- structural continuity

---

## Important Result

Inheritance reuse recovered successfully:

- inheritance_reuse_rate:
  ~0.46

This restored adaptive lineage persistence without returning to frozen inheritance dominance.

Additionally:

- coevolution_balance_score:
  ~0.73

showed strong balancing between:

- adaptive novelty
- inherited topology reuse

---

## Stability

The system preserved:

- continuity stability
- high structural diversity
- recovery capability
- bounded variance
- bounded trust dynamics

No collapse behavior was observed.

No runaway inheritance lock occurred.

---

## Remaining Issue

The system still failed strict validation.

The architecture approached stability, but adaptive novelty pressure remained slightly elevated and final balancing conditions were not fully satisfied.

This version is therefore preserved as:

experimental balancing attempt

rather than official stable completion.

---

## Significance

v18.5.1 demonstrated that:

- inheritance dominance can be reduced safely
- adaptive novelty can coexist with continuity
- topology persistence can remain stable during co-evolution balancing

This experiment became an important intermediate step toward fully stable environmental co-evolution.

---

## Future Direction

Next stabilization phase should focus on:

- slight novelty suppression
- continuity reinforcement
- finer inheritance decay balancing
- adaptive coevolution stabilization

while maintaining:

- distributed compatibility
- lightweight execution
- local adaptation
- recovery-safe exploration
